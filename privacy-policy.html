Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

1. Overview

Waymaker Auto Services LLC ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when you use our services or visit our website.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • City and state
  • Vehicle preferences and budget information
  • Trade-in vehicle information
  • Payment information (processed securely through Square — we do not store card details)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to provide our car buying consulting services, including:

  • Communicating with you about your vehicle search
  • Sourcing and presenting vehicle options
  • Negotiating with dealerships on your behalf
  • Processing your payment
  • Sending service-related emails and updates

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services which may collect or process your information:

  • Square — for payment processing. View their privacy policy at squareup.com
  • Google Forms — for our intake and referral forms. View Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com
  • Calendly — for scheduling consultations. View their privacy policy at calendly.com
  • Gmail — for email communications

5. Data Retention

We retain client communications and records for a minimum of 6 months following the completion of services. Records related to disputes or refund requests are retained for a minimum of 1 year.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information (subject to legal and business retention requirements)

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at waymakerautoservices@gmail.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
