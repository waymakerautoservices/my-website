Refund Policy

Last updated: May 2025

Overview

At Waymaker Auto Services LLC, we are committed to delivering real value to every client. We want our refund policy to be completely transparent and easy to understand — no fine print, no surprises.

All refund requests must be submitted within 30 days of the original purchase date by emailing us at waymakerautoservices@gmail.com. Refund requests submitted after 30 days will not be eligible for consideration.

Payment Processing Fee

Please note: All payments are processed through Square. Square's payment processing fee (3.3% + $0.30 per transaction) is non-recoverable — meaning Square retains this fee even when a refund is issued. As a result, all refunds will be issued minus Square's non-recoverable processing fee of approximately $16.70. This applies to all refund tiers including the pre-intake full refund. We want to be fully transparent about this so there are no surprises.

Our Sourcing & Research Process

Important: Waymaker Auto Services LLC conducts vehicle sourcing and research for every client — including those who come to us with a specific vehicle already in mind. Even if a client believes they have already found their ideal vehicle, we conduct independent research to ensure they are not missing a better deal on the same or comparable vehicle elsewhere. This sourcing and research process is a core part of our service and is performed for every client without exception.

Written Approval Defined

Written approval means a clear confirmation sent by the client via text message or email explicitly authorizing Waymaker Auto Services LLC to move forward with the negotiation and/or inspection stage for a specific vehicle that we have sourced and presented as the best available option. Written approval may include but is not limited to responses such as "yes," "go ahead," "I approve," or any other clear affirmative response to our sourced vehicle presentation.

Verbal approvals given over the phone do not constitute written approval. In all cases where a client provides verbal approval by phone, Waymaker Auto Services LLC will follow up with a written confirmation request via text or email, and work will not proceed to negotiation/inspection until written confirmation is received.

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please email us at waymakerautoservices@gmail.com with the subject line "Refund Request" and include:

  • Your full name
  • The email address used at purchase
  • Your purchase date
  • The date and time you submitted your intake form (if applicable)
  • The reason for your refund request

We will review your request and respond within 2 business days to confirm eligibility and the applicable refund amount.

Refund Processing Time

Once a refund is approved, please allow 5–7 business days for the refund to be processed and returned to your original payment method.

Deals Falling Through

In the car buying process, deals can and do fall through — a vehicle may be sold to another buyer, a seller may withdraw, or other unforeseen circumstances may prevent a transaction from completing. A deal falling through does not constitute grounds for a refund if written approval to proceed with negotiation/inspection has already been given. In such cases, Waymaker Auto Services LLC will continue working with the client to find a suitable alternative vehicle at no additional charge.
